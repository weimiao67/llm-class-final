## Differences between ZHVI and ZORI

ZHVI measures typical home values; ZORI measures typical market rents. They come from different
data: ZHVI from estimated values of all homes (Zestimates), ZORI from rental listings with a
repeat-rent method. Their units differ (a home value in dollars versus a monthly rent in dollars),
and they cover different periods (ZHVI here from 2000, ZORI from 2015) and different sets of
cities.

## Comparing the two indices

Compare their percentage changes over the same period and place rather than their dollar levels:
for example, "home values rose 20% and rents rose 15% from 2019 to 2024 in the same city". A
rent-to-price ratio or other combined measure is not one of this project's supported operations.
Historical association between the two is not evidence that one causes or predicts the other.
